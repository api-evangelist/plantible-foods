## What the company does

Plantible was founded in 2018 by Dutch entrepreneurs Tony and Maurits. Its production model is
vertically integrated: lemna is cultivated in automated greenhouse aquafarms, processed with a
proprietary water-based method, and scaled through modular expansion. The first commercial
facility is in Schleicher County, Texas.

Lemna doubles in mass roughly every 48 hours, grows without pesticides, and yields substantially
more protein per acre than soy at a fraction of the water use.

In February 2026 Plantible became the first company to receive an FDA "no questions" GRAS letter
for the use of isolated RuBisCO protein in food.
